The brief

Healthcare waits for something to break.

Annual check-ups test a handful of markers against population averages. Most people learn about heart disease, diabetes or cancer after symptoms show up, when it’s hardest to change course.

Telo works on the decades before that.

The brand had to make serious medicine feel calm, optimistic and personal. It also had to earn trust from members and from the clinics and employers who buy it.

The idea

One ring,
three meanings.

The name comes from telomeres, the caps that protect the ends of our chromosomes and shorten as we age. The logo’s open ring, sealed by a lime dot, carries that story into every touchpoint.

A protective cap

Telomeres guard our DNA. Telo guards your healthspan.

A progress ring

The ring closes as your biomarkers improve. The dot marks the goal.

A clock face

Time is the one thing we all spend. Telo helps you get more of it.
