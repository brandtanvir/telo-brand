    Good to know

    What is a “Telo age”?

    Your Telo age is a biological-age estimate built from 40+ validated biomarkers and functional tests. Unlike your calendar age, you can move it, and most members do within two retests.

    Is the full-body MRI safe?

    Yes. MRI uses magnetic fields, not radiation. Scans are read by two board-certified radiologists and reviewed with you by your Telo clinician.

    Does Telo replace my doctor?

    No. Telo works alongside your primary care. With your consent we share results and plans with your doctor, so everyone works from the same picture.

    Where is Telo available?

    Telo is live across the US, UK and EU, with 2,400 draw sites and 180 imaging partners. At-home draws are available in most metro areas.

    How is my data protected?

    End-to-end encryption, HIPAA and GDPR compliance, SOC 2 Type II audits. We never sell data, and you can export or delete it anytime.
